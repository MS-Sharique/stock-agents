Snapshot

Tata Power Company Ltd (NSE/BSE: TATAPOWER, BSE code 500400) is India's largest integrated private-sector power utility, spanning generation (thermal, hydro, renewables), transmission, distribution, rooftop solar, EV charging, and solar cell/module manufacturing.

Serious red flag up front: A Singapore court very recently (late August 2026) dismissed all of Tata Power's appeals against a Kleros Capital Partners arbitration award.

Tata Power lost a challenge to a $490 million arbitration award for violating an agreement with an investor to bid for a coal asset in Russia, denying it the opportunity to benefit.

Tata Power is now required to pay Kleros a sum exceeding USD 640 million, comprising principal damages plus interest accruing at 5.33% per annum since November 2020, plus legal costs.

This is a fresh, material, cash-liability event (roughly ₹5,300-5,600 crore at current FX), not a routine legal footnote.
